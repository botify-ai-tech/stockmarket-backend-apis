# 📊 Royal Orchid Hotels
## 💰 Summary
*   The company's standalone total revenue increased from operations, rising from ₹16,994.62 lakhs in FY 2023 to <font color='green'>₹19,193.62</font> lakhs in FY 2024.
*   Consolidated revenue from operations also increased from ₹26,354.88 lakhs in FY 2023 to <font color='green'>₹29,361.05</font> lakhs in FY 2024.
*   Standalone net profit decreased from ₹2,817.60 lakhs in FY 2023 to <font color='red'>₹2,462.03</font> lakhs in FY 2024.
*   Consolidated net profit increased from ₹4,922.31 lakhs in FY 2023 to <font color='green'>₹6,082.35</font> lakhs in FY 2024.
*   Standalone basic EPS decreased from ₹10.27 in FY 2023 to <font color='red'>₹8.98</font> in FY 2024.
*   Consolidated basic EPS increased from ₹17.15 in FY 2023 to <font color='green'>₹17.68</font> in FY 2024.
*   The company's standalone total equity is <font color='green'>₹23,447.36</font> lakhs and consolidated total equity is <font color='green'>₹20,810.30</font> lakhs as of March 31, 2024.
*   Auditor's opinion is qualified due to ongoing litigation related to KSDPL.

## 💰 Financial Health
*   **Revenue Growth:** Both standalone and consolidated revenues have increased. Standalone revenue grew by approximately 12.9%, from ₹16,994.62 lakhs to <font color='green'>₹19,193.62</font> lakhs. Consolidated revenue increased by approximately 11.4%, from ₹26,354.88 lakhs to <font color='green'>₹29,361.05</font> lakhs.
*   **Profitability:** While consolidated net profit saw an increase of approximately 23.6%, from ₹4,922.31 lakhs to <font color='green'>₹6,082.35</font> lakhs, the standalone net profit decreased by approximately 12.6%, from ₹2,817.60 lakhs to <font color='red'>₹2,462.03</font> lakhs. This divergence indicates that the subsidiaries or associate companies are contributing positively to the overall profitability.
*   **Expenses:** Employee benefits expense is a significant cost, amounting to ₹3,738.46 lakhs for standalone and ₹7,264.13 lakhs for consolidated financials. Food and beverage consumption expenses are also substantial at ₹1,948.10 lakhs for standalone and ₹2,914.77 lakhs for consolidated financials, which are approximately 10% of revenue.
*   **Debt Levels:** The standalone debt-to-equity ratio is approximately 0.22, and the consolidated debt-to-equity ratio is approximately 0.35. This suggests a relatively conservative approach to leveraging debt. Total standalone debt is ₹5,191.64 lakhs, while consolidated debt is ₹6,713.63 lakhs.
*   **Liquidity:** The standalone current ratio is 0.84, while the consolidated current ratio is 1.31. A current ratio below 1 for standalone financials may indicate potential short-term liquidity concerns, but the consolidated entity shows healthy short-term liquidity.
*   **Cash Flow:** Cash flow from operations is positive for both standalone (<font color='green'>₹4,716.28</font> lakhs) and consolidated (<font color='green'>₹5,939.41</font> lakhs) financials, indicating the company's ability to generate cash from its core business activities. However, both standalone and consolidated entities experienced a net decrease in cash and cash equivalents.

## 📈 Investment Insights
*   **Strengths:** The company demonstrates revenue growth at both the standalone and consolidated levels. The consolidated entity exhibits increased profitability and a healthy current ratio. Positive cash flow from operations also suggests a stable core business.
*   **Weaknesses:** The decrease in standalone net profit and EPS, coupled with a current ratio below 1, raises concerns about standalone financial performance and short-term liquidity.
*   **Risks:** The qualified audit opinion due to ongoing litigation related to KSDPL is a significant risk. Any adverse outcome from the NCLT petition or SEBI order could negatively impact the company's financials. The increased finance costs in both standalone and consolidated financials could strain profitability.
*   **Opportunities:** The acquisition of the remaining shares of Icon Hospitality Private Limited, making it a wholly-owned subsidiary, presents an opportunity for enhanced synergies and profitability in the future. The company's focus on the hospitality business (as its single operating segment) may allow for specialized growth and operational efficiencies.
